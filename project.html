About the Project

Res Hub is a Student Off-Campus Residence Review and Reporting System designed to help students make informed decisions when choosing student accommodation.

The system allows students to access information and reviews about student residences from other students. This provides students with useful information about accommodation before making a decision.

Problem Statement

Students often have difficulty finding honest and reliable information about off-campus residences. Information provided by landlords may focus mainly on the positive aspects of a residence, while students may not have enough information about problems such as maintenance, security, infrastructure and available services.

Res Hub aims to address this problem by providing a platform where students can share their experiences and review residences.

Main functionalities of the system

  • Allow students to search for residences.
  • Allow students to rate residences.
  • Allow students to write residence reviews.
  • Allow students to view residence information.
  • Allow students to upload and view photos.
  • Allow students to rate residence services.
  • Allow students to like useful reviews.
  • Allow administrators to manage inappropriate content.

Main Features

  1. Residence Search
  2. Residence Ratings
  3. Student Reviews
  4. Residence Photos
  5. Service Ratings
  6. Review Likes
  7. Administrator Management
